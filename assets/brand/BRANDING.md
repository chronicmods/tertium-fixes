# Tertium Fixes branding

These original community-mod assets use a broken mechanical diagnostic ring
closed by an amber-to-teal repair seam. The mark represents targeted client-side
repairs, safe cleanup, and restored state without implying an official game
overhaul.

## Assets

- `tertium-fixes-hero.jpg` - 1600 x 900 release and repository artwork
- `tertium-fixes-banner.jpg` - 1600 x 400 Nexus and social banner
- `tertium-fixes-icon-512.png` - 512 x 512 thumbnail and avatar mark

## Palette

- Soot: `#11161A`
- Gunmetal: `#24272A`
- Bone: `#D8D2C4`
- Oxidized brass: `#8B6A3E`
- Repair amber: `#D58A28`
- Monitor teal: `#44B7B5`

The artwork deliberately avoids official game, publisher, mod-framework, and
storefront logos or faction marks. Tertium Fixes is an unofficial community mod.

## Generation prompt set

The assets were generated with the built-in ImageGen workflow using these final
creative directions:

- **Hero:** Original 16:9 gothic-industrial science-fiction maintenance bay; a
  fractured mechanical diagnostic ring repaired by one amber-to-teal seam; four
  subtle channels for interface, input, audio/effects, and memory cleanup; exact
  text `TERTIUM FIXES` and `CLIENT-SIDE REPAIRS & CLEANUP`; restrained cinematic
  realism; no official logos, insignia, characters, weapons, gore, copied UI,
  version number, performance claims, or watermark.
- **Banner:** Coordinated ultra-wide industrial banner with the repaired ring on
  the right and a dark title-safe area on the left; exact text `TERTIUM FIXES`
  and `CLIENT-SIDE REPAIRS & CLEANUP`; sparse circuitry and status lights; no
  official marks, characters, copied UI, claims, or watermark.
- **Icon:** Square, vector-friendly broken mechanical ring/hex-shield emblem
  closed by a luminous repair seam with a small status-check notch; no text,
  letters, version, mockup, official marks, characters, weapons, or watermark.
